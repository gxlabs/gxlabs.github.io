        <h3 class="mt-0"><a href="/bean">Bean: coffee reviews</a></h3>
        <p>A coffee log for people who care which bag they're brewing. Rate every cup, find your next bag, and see what your friends are drinking.</p>
        <ul class="text-left">
            <li>Log a cup in seconds: rating, brew method, tasting notes, a photo, and where you bought the bag.</li>
            <li>Dial in espresso: dose, yield, grind and grinder on every cup, prefilled from your last cup of the same bean. Your machine and grinder live in My gear.</li>
            <li>Track the bag: roast date with days off roast, price and size, and your price per 100&nbsp;g.</li>
            <li>Find beans by roast, process, decaf, rating, what you haven't tried, your want-to-try list, or what people buy near you.</li>
            <li>Every bean shows its origin, process and variety, how it rates per brew method, and your own rating next to everyone's.</li>
            <li>Add a roaster from its website and Bean finds the coffees it sells; share a product page from Safari and Bean adds it.</li>
            <li>Follow friends, like and comment on their cups, and choose who sees each review: everyone, people who follow you, or just you.</li>
            <li>Your stats: cups this month, beans and roasters tried, your brew methods and top tastes. Export everything, photos included, as a ZIP.</li>
        </ul>
        <p>Download on the <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6821039584">iOS App Store</a>.</p>
