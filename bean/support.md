Please contact: bean-coffee-reviews-support@gxlabs.co

**Reporting content or people**

Open the review, comment, or profile, tap the **•••** menu (or long-press a comment), and choose **Report**. Reports are reviewed and objectionable content is removed.

**Deleting your account**

In the app, go to **Profile → Settings → Delete account**. This permanently removes your profile, reviews, comments, and likes. You can also email the address above to ask for your account to be deleted.
